ONBOARDING

One account.
Your choice of roles.

Pick buyer, pick seller, or set up both — the roles live side by side on a single account, so switching later never means a second identity. Everything shown here is the account service's own state: completion, requirements, and next steps are read back from the server, never computed by this page.

Sign in to see your live role status from the account service. The role cards below stay usable as direct links either way.

Buyer

Digital purchases only: your name and referral answer, plus verified email from the real verification mechanism. No address, no phone number, no identity document.

Not started Set up as buyer

Seller

Creator identity — handle, display name, bio, compatibility, referral, and the creator agreement. Verification and publishing are separate states, not part of this form.

Not started Set up as seller

Buyer and seller never conflict: they are two records on the same account, and completing one does not lock, publish, or approve anything on the other.

Verification, stated plainly

Each mechanism exists or does not — the site never blurs the line.

  • Email — verified by the account service's existing verification mechanism.
  • Phone — verification does not exist in CraftMind yet, so no phone number is collected or presented as verified.
  • Creator verification — a developer-controlled marker, not identity or KYC review.
  • Trusted Seller and publishing — separate, later capabilities; onboarding never grants them.
