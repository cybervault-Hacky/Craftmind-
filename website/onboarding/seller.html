What this form does not do

Stated up front so a completed profile is never mistaken for something more.

  • It does not verify identity: creator verification is a developer-controlled marker, never KYC or identity review.
  • It does not publish: marketplace publishing is not implemented yet, and completing this profile grants no listing capability.
  • It does not pay out: no price, payout destination, or financial detail exists in this phase.
  • It does not grant plans: membership and entitlements come from CraftMind, never from this form.
