ACCOUNT ACCESS

Sign in,
or start fresh.

A dedicated account experience for CraftMind: sign in, create a free account, and verify your email through the account service's existing mechanism. One account later holds both roles — buyer and seller — and this page improves nothing by pretending: no fake login, no client-side secrets, no invented status.

What an account is for

Free to create, and only as much as the account service actually implements.

BUYER AND SELLER, ONE LOGIN

Choose both roles later

After signing in you pick a first role — buyer or seller — and you can hold both. No second account, no second password, and neither role costs anything to activate.

HONEST LIMITS

What is not here

No payment method, no purchase, and no phone verification exists in this phase. Multi-factor authentication and account deletion are not implemented, and this page never stores credentials in browser storage.
