HOW HIRING WORKS HERE

A request, a proposal,
an informed choice.

STEP 1 · BUYER

Post the job

Describe the build, the single target version, loaders, optional budget range, and deadline. Your job appears in the open directory while it is OPEN, and you can edit or cancel it before choosing a builder.

STEP 2 · BUILDER

Send one clear proposal

Signed-in creators with an active profile can send a single live proposal per job — message, scope, optional budget, and delivery estimate. Only you and the buyer ever read your proposal.

STEP 3 · BUYER

Award the job

Review every proposal side by side and award exactly one builder. An award records your choice and closes the rest — no payment, escrow, or milestone exists in this phase, and none is implied here.
