MARKETPLACE ORDERS

Your orders,
both sides of the work.

After a buyer awards a proposal, the order that follows freezes the agreed terms and tracks every milestone from start to approval. You see the same order here whichever side you are on — as the buyer who approves, or as the builder who delivers.

TRACK

Real terms, real milestones, real history.

Every card here comes from your own session on the account service: the terms frozen when the order was created, the milestone counts, and the role you hold. Nothing on this page is sample data.

NOT YET SUPPORTED

Payment processing, escrow, refunds, and payouts are not yet supported: an order tracks agreed work between two accounts, not money. Order records are workflow records only — they are not proof that any payment happened.
