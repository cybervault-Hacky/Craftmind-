WORKS TODAY

Search, filters, sorting, and saved state

The interface is real and keyboard accessible. Saving an item keeps it in this page for this visit only — nothing is uploaded, and nothing is written to browser storage.

CHECKOUT NOT IMPLEMENTED

Purchases and downloads

Listings are stored, searched, and served when an account service is connected — drafts and archived rows stay private. No checkout exists, and no build can be purchased or downloaded. Purchase controls are shown as unavailable rather than faked.
