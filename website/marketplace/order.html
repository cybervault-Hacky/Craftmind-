ORDER DETAIL

Terms frozen.
Progress visible.

This page reads one order from the account service: the agreed scope and terms exactly as they were when the order was created, every milestone with the actions your role allows, and the delivery and revision history both parties share. The service decides every state — the page never does.

AGREED WORK

One order, two roles, one history.

Builders start milestones and submit delivery versions; buyers approve or request revisions; completion needs every milestone approved. Approved milestones never reopen, and the revision limit is finite and agreed at creation.

NOT YET SUPPORTED

Payment processing, escrow, refunds, and payouts are not yet supported: an order tracks agreed work between two accounts, not money. Order records are workflow records only — they are not proof that any payment happened.
