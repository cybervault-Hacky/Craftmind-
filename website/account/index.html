ACCOUNT CENTER

Your account,
without the guesswork.

Sign-in, email verification, password recovery, password change, and session control are implemented in the CraftMind account service. Purchases, memberships, saved builds, and cloud sync are not — and each page here says which is which instead of showing an empty screen.

What exists today

Stated from the implemented service surface, not from a roadmap.

IMPLEMENTED

Authentication and sessions

Registration, email verification, sign-in, refresh, sign-out, session listing, and session revocation against a configured account service. Multi-factor authentication is not implemented.

NOT IMPLEMENTED

Purchases, memberships, and sync

No marketplace, purchase history, subscription, credit, or cross-device sync exists. Account deletion is unavailable, and this site never displays an internal account identifier.
