MEMBERSHIP · NOT YET SELLABLE

Plans that say
what they are.

CraftMind is free today. A Pro, Creator, and Server tier are designed but not built, so no price is final and nothing can be purchased, upgraded, downgraded, or cancelled. Every plan below states its own status instead of implying a subscription that does not exist.

PLANS

Four tiers, one honest status.

Pricing is deliberately absent. A later phase defines the numbers; this phase defines the experience around them.

Billing period

Billing, explained honestly

Billing

No billing exists. CraftMind does not store a payment method, does not create invoices, does not charge a card, and does not integrate any payment provider in this phase.

Upgrading and downgrading

Upgrade and downgrade controls are part of the planned interface. They are rendered as unavailable, because a control that appears to change a plan would be a lie about a service that does not exist.

Cancellation

There is nothing to cancel. When subscriptions exist, cancellation will be self-service and the page will state the exact date access ends — CraftMind will not hide a cancellation path.

What a plan will never change

Membership will not change how builds are validated, whether a plan can be reviewed before it runs, or the safety limits on Minecraft execution. Those are product guarantees, not paid features.

Is CraftMind paid right now?

No. The app is free to use with your own AI provider key, and no paid tier exists. Nothing on this page can charge you.

Why show plans that cannot be bought?

Because the product experience is designed before the billing system. Every plan is labelled with its real status, including “planned — not purchasable”, and no price is invented to make the page look finished.

What will a plan include?

The capability lists above are the intended shape: higher generation limits and advanced AI building for Pro, creator tools and marketplace selling for Creator, and server/team features for Server. Limits and credits are not defined, so no number is promised here.

Will prices change?

Prices do not exist yet. When they do, they will be configurable service-side values rather than values baked into this page.
